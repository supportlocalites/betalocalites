# Try every feature on your phone

A click-through for the owner, on the test site, using your own phone. Tick each line as you go,
and note anything confusing, slow or broken (what you did, the time, the page address).
Written 18 October 2026. About 60–90 minutes in total; you can do it in parts.

**You'll need:** your phone; a second email address for a pretend customer (a Gmail address with
`+test` added before the @, like yourname+test@gmail.com, arrives in the same inbox); your team
account (password and authenticator app); a computer or second browser helps for the team steps.

## 1. As a visitor (not signed in), 10 minutes

- [ ] Open the test site. The Welcome page shows the Localites name with the map pin.
- [ ] **Explore**: open a kind of business, then a business's page.
- [ ] Search for a word (for example "cake"); try a word that finds nothing.
- [ ] On a business page, tap **Visit website** and **Call** (you can hang up).
- [ ] At the bottom of any page, the footer: open **Help**, **How it works**, **For businesses**.
- [ ] On a business page, tap **Something wrong with this listing? Tell us**: an email opens to the team with the page's address.
- [ ] **Suggest a business** with a made-up name.
- [ ] Send yourself a business page's link in WhatsApp or Messages: a preview with the name and a picture shows.

## 2. As a customer, 30 minutes (use the +test email)

- [ ] **Sign in**: type the email, enter the 6-digit code from your inbox, give a first and last name.
- [ ] **Your account**: check your details; untick and re-tick the "How was it?" emails box.
- [ ] **Plan an event**: give it a name, date (in the past week, so reviews open), town and guests.
- [ ] From a business page, **Add to event**, and **Save** another business.
- [ ] On a claimed business, **Message** it, and attach a photo.
- [ ] On a business that hasn't joined, **Inquire**.
- [ ] On your plan, **Mark as booked** a claimed business. (The business's owner gets a summary email the next morning.)
- [ ] **Guests**: add two guests, one with your own email. Send one their link by text to yourself and open it: reply **Yes**, and **Add to calendar**.
- [ ] Make the **group link**, open it in another browser, and reply with a name.
- [ ] Ask your guests a question and post an update; see them on the invitation.
- [ ] **Print** the group link's QR code page (or just look at it); scan the code with your camera.
- [ ] **Review** the business you marked booked (it waits for the team).
- [ ] **Your account → Download your data**: both the readable copy and the data file download; the readable copy opens in your browser.

## 3. As a business owner, 20 minutes (your own email)

- [ ] On a business you can vouch for, **Claim this business**. (Approve it yourself in step 4, then come back.)
- [ ] **Edit your listing**: change the description and save; it shows straight away.
- [ ] Type a rude word in the description and save: it's held for the team, not shown.
- [ ] **Photos**: add one from your camera roll; choose the profile photo and frame it.
- [ ] **Messages**: reply to the customer's message.
- [ ] **Bookings marked**: see the customer's booking; try **This isn't right** on it.
- [ ] **Reviews**: once the review is approved (step 4), reply to it once.
- [ ] **Team**: invite your +test email as a manager; accept it from that inbox.
- [ ] **Analytics** (after the team switches it on in step 4): numbers show for your business.

## 4. As the team, 20 minutes (computer or second browser)

- [ ] **Sign in** at /sign-in with your password and authenticator app.
- [ ] **Waiting for you**: everything from steps 1–3 is listed.
- [ ] **Claims**: approve your claim from step 3, ticking how you checked.
- [ ] **Held changes**: decline the rude save with a reason; the owner's email arrives.
- [ ] **Reviews**: approve the customer's review (try **Deny** on another if you have one).
- [ ] **Suggestions**: decline the made-up business.
- [ ] Each listing's **Activity** page: switch on Analytics for your business.
- [ ] **Accounts**: find the +test customer by email and **Suspend** them. On the phone, as the customer, open any page: it says the account is suspended. Then **Lift** it.
- [ ] **Audit log**: your actions are listed with your name.

## 5. Tidy up, 5 minutes

- [ ] As the customer: **Your account → Delete your account**.
- [ ] As the team: **Deleted accounts** shows it "to finish"; follow the steps there.
- [ ] The next morning: check the business's inbox for the booking summary, and the team's inbox for the daily note.

**Send the owner's notes** (what was confusing, slow or broken) to the developer, with the time and the page address for each.
