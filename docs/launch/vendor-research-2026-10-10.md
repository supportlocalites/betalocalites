# Launch vendor research

**Researched:** 10 October 2026, for PO-05 / PO-08 / D-03.
**Result:** 61 Orillia businesses and 17 nearby (Severn, Oro-Medonte, Ramara) for later.
A public rating was visible for 14 of 78.

**How this was done.** Web searches only, one researcher per category. Web pages couldn't be opened
from the research environment, and search results rarely show Google ratings, so most ratings are
"not seen" and need a check on Google Maps. Nothing here is invented: every business, link and
rating comes from a search result, and anything uncertain is marked. Ratings are only our private
shortlist filter (D-03); they're never copied to or shown on Localites. Only business information is
recorded (no owners' personal names, home addresses or personal phone numbers).

**Status:** *Looks active* = recent signs it's operating · *Check first* = real, but the town, events
service or activity needs confirming · *Doubtful* = may not exist any more.

The product owner picks from this list on the shortlist page; picks are recorded in H (PO-08).
