## 1. Who we are

Localites is a website that helps people in Orillia, Ontario and the surrounding area find
local businesses for their events and contact them directly. "Localites", "we" and "us" mean
[legal name of the business owner or company], the operator of localites.ca.

**Privacy contact:** supportlocalites@gmail.com
**Mailing address:** [to be added before launch (D-12)]

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who can see it, how long we
keep it, and the choices you have. It applies to localites.ca and the emails we send.

## 2. The short version

- We collect what we need to run Localites, plus a record of how people use the site so we
  can improve it and give businesses totals about their own page.
- **Businesses never see who viewed them.** They see totals only. They see your name only
  when you message them, mark them as booked, or review them.
- **We don't sell personal information, show ads, or use outside advertising trackers.**
- **Localites doesn't handle payments.** We never ask for card or bank details.
- You can see and change your details, turn off optional emails, and delete your account at
  any time from **Your account**.

## 3. What we collect

### When you create an account
- **Required:** your first and last name, and your email address. We email you a 6-digit
  code each time you sign in; there's no password.
- **Optional:** gender, date of birth and street address. Fill in only what
  you're happy to share. You can change or clear these at any time on **Your account**.

### When you plan an event
- What you enter about the event: its name, kind of event, date, start and end time, town,
  place, number of guests, age range, budget and notes.
- The businesses you save, consider, or mark as booked, and the reviews you write.

### When you use Community
- The questions and answers you post, any business you suggest in an answer, your votes (up or
  down) and any reports you send. Who voted is never shown; only the totals on each answer.

### When you invite guests
- The names you enter for your guests, and, if you choose, their email address or phone
  number, so you can send each guest their own link.
- Guests' replies (yes, no or maybe, how many are coming, a note, and answers to any
  questions you ask, which might include things like allergies). A guest who replies through
  a group link gives their name.
- **If you add someone's contact details, please make sure they're happy for you to.** We
  email a guest in only two cases: when you change their reply, we tell them what changed; and
  when you cancel an event and ask us to, we tell them it's cancelled.

### When you contact a business
- **Messages and files** you send in a conversation (photos and PDFs), and the business's
  replies. If either of you edits a message, its earlier versions are kept and both of you can
  see them.
- **Inquiries** about a business that isn't on Localites yet: which business, your note,
  and, if you choose, a summary of your event. These go to the Localites team, not to the
  business.

### When you suggest a business
- The business's name and, if you know them, its website and a phone number or email. If
  you're signed in, the suggestion is linked to your account so we can tell you when the
  business is listed. To stop one connection sending too many in a day, we keep a coded
  fingerprint of your internet connection for that day (never the address itself), erased
  after 2 days.

### If you run a business on Localites
- Your business's details: name, description, contact details, photos and services. Most of this is public on your business page.
- Who is on your business's team (owner, managers and staff), and the details you give when
  you claim a listing.
- Your replies to reviews, your messages with customers, and any booking you tell us isn't
  right.

### Businesses not on Localites yet
- We list some businesses before they join, using only the details each business publishes
  itself (its own website and public listings). These pages say "Not on Localites yet". A
  business can ask us to remove its listing at any time by emailing
  supportlocalites@gmail.com.

### How the site is used (activity records)
- **What:** which business pages are viewed and for how long, which photos are opened, taps
  on a business's website, phone, email, directions or social links, where a business
  appeared in a list, **the words typed into search**, and saves, inquiries and messages.
- **With each action:** the kind of device (phone, tablet or computer), your approximate town
  and province (worked out by our hosting provider from your connection; we don't keep your
  internet address), the name of the website that sent you to Localites, and the date and
  time.
- **Linked to:** a random visitor number kept in a cookie (see section 7) or, when you're
  signed in, your account.

### Technical information
- **Internet (IP) address:** used for a few minutes to slow down anyone sending too many
  requests, and passed to Cloudflare's "are you human?" check on the sign-in page. Localites
  doesn't store it with your account or your activity. [To confirm: whether Supabase's
  sign-in service keeps addresses in its own logs, and for how long.]
- Records of errors on the site, kept by our hosting provider for a short time. [To confirm:
  how long Cloudflare keeps these on our plan, and that they hold no message content.]

## 4. How we use it

- **To run Localites:** sign you in, show your events, plans, guest lists and messages, and
  send the emails described in section 6.
- **To connect you with businesses:** pass on what you choose to share with them (see
  section 5).
- **To keep Localites safe and fair:** check that reviews come from real bookings, look into
  reports, stop spam and abuse, and keep a record of every change the team makes.
- **To check words before they show:** what businesses write on their page, reviews,
  businesses' replies to reviews, and Community questions and answers are checked for offensive language before they appear,
  first by our own word list and then by an automated service (Cloudflare's AI service).
  Reviews, replies and Community posts that seem to include a phone number, email address or street address
  are held too, so personal details aren't posted by mistake. Anything flagged is held for a
  person on our team to decide. Private messages are not checked this way.
- **To improve Localites and report to businesses:** we use activity records to understand
  how the site is used, and give each business totals about its own page (section 5). The
  optional details on your account (gender, date of birth, address) may be used, combined with
  others', for the same purpose. [Lawyer: is this purpose described clearly enough, and is
  it acceptable to use optional profile details for it?]

## 5. Who sees what

| Who | What they see |
|---|---|
| **Other people using Localites** | Your published reviews, and your Community questions and answers, shown with your first name and the first letter of your last name (for example "Sarah M."). Anyone can read Community, signed in or not. Never how you voted or what you reported. Nothing else about you. |
| **A business you contact** | Your name and what you write or send in a conversation. Never your email address unless you type it. If you link an event to a conversation, they see a short summary: the date, the town and about how many guests. |
| **A business you mark as booked** | Your name, the date, the town and about how many guests. Never your event's name, kind of event, start time, place, ages, budget, notes, the other businesses on your plan, or your email address. They can tell us if a booking isn't right. |
| **A business you review** | Your review, shown as above, and they may reply once. |
| **Businesses (in general)** | Totals only about their own page: how often it was seen, viewed and tapped; inquiries, conversations, messages and how quickly they replied. **Never who.** Businesses don't see saves, plans or bookings as totals at all. |
| **Your guests** | Your first name, the event details you choose to show on the invitation, your updates and your questions. Never your other guests, your notes or your email address. |
| **The Localites team** | What's needed to run the service: account details, claims, inquiries, suggestions, reviews waiting for approval, and the detailed activity records. The team reads a conversation only if someone reports it, and every such look is recorded. |
| **Anyone else** | Nothing, except what's public: business pages and published reviews. |

## 6. Emails we send

- **Sign-in codes**, when you ask for one.
- **Messages:** a note when someone sends you a message and you haven't seen it yet (at most
  one for each unread stretch).
- **For businesses:** a morning summary of bookings (marked as booked, no longer booked, or an
  event changed or cancelled), claim decisions, team invitations, and notices about your
  page and reviews.
- **About your reviews:** when your review is published, and when the business replies to it.
- **About reports:** the outcome of a report you sent (never the team's notes).
- **After an event:** a "How was it?" email asking you to review the businesses you booked,
  and one reminder. You can turn these off on **Your account**.
- **News you asked for:** that a business you inquired about or suggested is now on
  Localites.
- **About a booking a business says isn't right:** when the business tells us, and our team's
  decision (to you and to the business); and to guests whose reply their host changed, or
  whose event their host cancelled and asked us to tell them (section 3).

We don't send newsletters or marketing emails. [Lawyer: please confirm these all count as

## 7. Cookies and similar technology

Localites uses only its own cookies. There are no outside advertising or tracking cookies.

| Cookie | What it's for | How long |
|---|---|---|
| `__Host-lc_at`, `__Host-lc_rt` | Keep you signed in | 30 days, or until you sign out |
| `__Host-lc_vid` | A random visitor number for activity records (section 3), so a returning visitor is counted once and your own visits to your business are left out of its totals | 1 year |
| `__Host-localites-invite`, `__Host-localites-group` | Open an invitation without keeping its link in your browser's address bar | 1 day |
| `__Host-localites-gkey` | Lets the same browser change a reply made through a group link | 60 days |

The site also keeps a short note in your browser while a page is open, so it only counts
activity from real browsers. It's cleared when you close the tab.

## 8. Service providers and where your information is kept

We use a few outside services to run Localites. Each one handles information only to provide
its service to us.

| Provider | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Our database, sign-in service and file storage (business photos, files in conversations) | Canada (Central region) |
| **Cloudflare** | Hosts the site, runs the "are you human?" check, limits repeated requests, works out approximate town, and runs the automated word check (section 4) | Worldwide network, including outside Canada |
| **Resend** | Sends our emails (your email address, and the email's content) | United States |
| **GitHub** | Stores our encrypted nightly backup for 30 days | United States |
| **OpenFreeMap** | Draws the maps on business pages and Browse local: your browser fetches the map's data from it directly, so it sees your internet address and which page you're on (no account, nothing else from Localites) | Worldwide |
| **OpenStreetMap** (OpenStreetMap Foundation) | The map's information comes from OpenStreetMap, and when OpenFreeMap can't be reached your browser fetches OpenStreetMap's own map pictures directly, so it sees your internet address and which page you're on (no account, nothing else from Localites). When a business saves its address, we ask its free address search where to put the pin: we send the street, number (never when the business hides it) and town, nothing about you | United Kingdom and worldwide |

**Directions** on a business's page open Google Maps in a new tab, only if you tap it; Google's
own privacy policy then applies. A business can hide its street number: it's then never shown
on Localites and no longer kept (our team's record of earlier changes, which can't be edited, still
has it if the business entered it before hiding it), and its pin is only near the street
("Approximate location").

Information processed outside Canada may be accessible to the authorities of those countries

## 10. Deleting your account

On **Your account → Delete your account**, after you type your email address, we immediately:

- delete your events, plans, guest lists, saved businesses and shortlist (so businesses you marked as
  booked no longer see the booking);
- erase your name, email address and personal details;
- remove you from any business team;
- unlink you from activity records and suggestions;
- show your messages as "Former Localites member" and delete the files you sent (unless
  they're part of a report); and
- show your published reviews as "A Localites member".

Within 30 days the team also removes your record from our sign-in service. Copies in our
backups are gone within 30 days.

- **See and change your details** on **Your account**.
- **Turn off "How was it?" emails** on **Your account**.
- **Ask for a copy** of the personal information we hold about you: **Download your data** on
  **Your account** gives you a readable copy and a data file straight away, including what you
  did for a business you're on the team of, or email supportlocalites@gmail.com for
  anything it doesn't include. [Lawyer: the file leaves out a review the team denied (D-51) and

- **Ask us to correct** anything that's wrong.
- **Delete your account** at any time (section 10).
- **Complain:** contact us first. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the
  Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).

## 13. How we protect your information

- Every page checks who you are on our server, and our database checks again, so one person
  can't see another's events, plans or messages.
- Invitation links are long random codes, and we store only a scrambled version of them.
- Photos have location details removed when they're uploaded.
- The Localites team signs in with a password and an authenticator app, and every change
  they make is recorded.
- No system is perfectly secure. If a breach could cause you significant harm, we'll tell
  you and the Privacy Commissioner, as the law requires.
