Reviews help people choose local businesses. This policy explains who can review, what's
allowed, how we check reviews, and what businesses can do.

## 1. Who can write a review

You can review a business when **all** of these are true:

- you planned the event on Localites and marked the business as booked for it;
- the event has happened, and it's within **60 days** of the event;
- the event wasn't cancelled;
- the business hasn't told us it wasn't booked for your event;
- it isn't your own business, or one you're on the team of; and
- the business is listed on Localites.

You can review each booking once. The day after your event, we email you to ask how it went,
and send one reminder; you can turn these emails off on **Your account**.

Each review is labelled **"Planned on Localites"** if the business replied to you in a
Localites conversation, or **"Planned outside Localites"** if it didn't.

## 2. What a review can include

- A rating from 1 to 5 stars and your words (up to 3,000 characters).
- Your honest experience of the business, good or bad.

## 3. What isn't allowed

- Reviews that aren't based on your own real experience, or that are about a different
  business or event.
- Reviews from the business itself, its staff, family or friends, or a competitor.
- Reviews written in exchange for anything: money, discounts, gifts or favours.
- Abusive, hateful, threatening, sexually explicit or offensive content.
- Someone's personal details (phone numbers, addresses, private information).
- Advertising, links to other businesses, or spam.

## 4. How we check reviews

- **Every review is checked by a person on our team before it appears.** The team approves
  it, and it goes on the business's page, or denies it, and it doesn't appear.
- Before that, an automated check (our own word list and Cloudflare's AI service) looks for
  offensive language and tells the team what it found. The team makes the decision.
- We don't edit reviews. We publish them as written or not at all.
- We don't publish, hide, rank or remove reviews because a business pays us or asks us to.
  Featured placements and any paid plans have no effect on reviews.
- If a review is denied, it doesn't appear anywhere, and the business isn't told about it.

## 5. How reviews are shown

- Each review shows the author's first name and the first letter of their last name (for
  example "Sarah M."), the rating, the words, the month of the event, and whether it was
  planned on Localites.
- If the author deletes their account, the review stays and shows as "A Localites member".
- Reviews show only while the business is listed on Localites.
- **Living Local:** reviewers in the top 1% by number of published reviews carry a "Living
  Local" badge. This starts only once Localites has 100 or more reviewers. The badge reflects
  how many reviews someone has written, nothing else.
- A business's average rating is worked out from all its published reviews.

## 6. What businesses can do

- **Reply once** to each review, publicly, under the review. Replies follow the same rules
  as reviews and may be held for our team if the automated check flags them.
- **Report a review** that breaks this policy, choosing a reason: not a real customer; from a
  competitor or someone too close to the business; shares personal details; abusive or
  offensive; or something else.
- **Tell us a booking isn't right** if someone marked the business as booked for an event it
  wasn't booked for. The team looks into it, and the person is told.

Businesses can't remove reviews, see who denied or approved them, or contact reviewers through
Localites to ask them to change a review.
