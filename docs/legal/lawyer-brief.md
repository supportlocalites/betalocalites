# Localites: brief for legal review

> For the lawyer reviewing Localites' policies before launch (PO-07). Prepared 18 October 2026.
> Attached: Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Review Policy (drafts). Open questions are also
> marked in **[square brackets]** inside each draft.

## What Localites is

A free website for Orillia, Ontario and area. People planning events (birthdays, weddings,
community events) find local businesses, save and compare them, message them, mark them as
booked on their own plan, invite guests, and review businesses after the event. Businesses
claim their page, reply to messages and reviews, and see totals about their page.

- **No payments of any kind go through the site**, in either direction. Any business plan or
  featured placement would be paid to the operator outside the site. None exist yet.
- Some businesses are listed before they join, from their own published details, marked "Not
  on Localites yet", and removed on request.
- Hosting is in Canada for the database and files (Supabase, Canada Central); Cloudflare
  (worldwide) hosts the site; Resend (United States) sends email; GitHub (United States) holds
  an encrypted nightly backup for 30 days.
- The operator is [legal name / sole proprietor or company]. Mailing address to be added.

## What we'd like you to review

1. **The three drafts:** accuracy of the wording against Canadian and Ontario law (PIPEDA,
   CASL, Ontario Consumer Protection Act, Competition Act), and anything missing.
2. **Children.** Anyone can create an account, with no minimum age; sign-up asks for (but
   doesn't require) date of birth, gender and street address. Do we need a minimum age, or a
   parent's consent under 13 or another age? Should we stop asking young users for these?
3. **Activity records with no end date.** We record page views, taps and the words people
   search, linked to a visitor cookie or account, kept indefinitely for year-over-year
   reports and unlinked when an account is deleted. Is "no set end date" acceptable for the
   stated purposes, or should we set a period (or anonymise after one)?
4. **Using optional profile details** (gender, date of birth, address) for improving the site
   and businesses' totals. Is the purpose stated clearly enough? Is consent adequate?
5. **Cookie notice.** We use only our own cookies, including a 1-year visitor-number cookie
   for activity records. Is a first-visit notice or consent needed?
6. **Emails and CASL.** Are these transactional: the "How was it?" email after an event (with
   one reminder, can be turned off), "a business you asked about is now on Localites", and a
   business's morning booking summary?
7. **Denied reviews (Review Policy §4).** Every review is approved or denied by a person; the
   owner chose that a denied review's author isn't told and sees no trace. Is that fair and
   compliant, in particular with the Competition Act's rules on reviews?
8. **Automated language check.** Business page edits, reviews and replies are checked by a word
   list and Cloudflare's AI service, and flagged items held for a person. Is the disclosure
   enough? (Private messages aren't checked.)
9. **Guests' details entered by hosts.** Hosts enter guests' names and optional emails or phone
   numbers; we email a guest only when the host changes their reply. Is the host's
   responsibility (Terms §5) enough?
10. **Listing businesses before they join**, from their own public details, with removal on
    request. Any concerns?
11. **Data outside Canada and Quebec.** Is the cross-border wording enough? Do we need anything
    for Quebec's Law 25 if Quebec residents use the site?
12. **Business name.** If "Localites" isn't a registered business name yet, does it need
    registering in Ontario before launch?
13. **Retention periods** for reported messages and files (currently kept with the report, no
    end date), and the disclaimer and liability section of the Terms (§10).

## Things to know about the drafts

- Each draft was written from what the site actually does, and checked against its code.
- A few promises are marked **(to be built before launch)**: automatic deletion of old
  conversations, email records, the team's change log and declined suggestions' contact
  details; a "Download my data" button; and the team's ability to suspend an account. These
  will be built before the policies are published.
- The owner will confirm the operator's legal name and mailing address.
