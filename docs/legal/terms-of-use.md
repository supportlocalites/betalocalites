## 2. What Localites is, and isn't

Localites helps people find local businesses for their events and contact them directly.

- **We connect you; we're not part of the deal.** Any arrangement you make with a business
  is between you and that business. Localites isn't a party to it and isn't responsible for
  the business's services, prices, quality, safety, timing or cancellations.
- **Localites doesn't process payments.** You arrange and pay for everything directly with
  the business. We'll never ask you for card or bank details, and nobody should ask you to
  pay "through Localites".
- **A "booked" mark is your own record.** Marking a business as booked on your plan helps you
  keep track. It isn't a contract or a confirmation from the business.
- **Business details come from businesses.** Pages marked "Not on Localites yet" show only
  what the business publishes itself. We try to keep listings accurate but can't promise
  they're complete or current. Check the important details with the business.
- **We don't check businesses for you** unless a page says otherwise. [Lawyer: if a
  "Verified" badge is used later, describe what it means here.]

## 4. Using Localites fairly

Don't use Localites to:

- harass, threaten, or abuse anyone, or post anything hateful, sexually explicit, or
  offensive;
- send spam, advertising nobody asked for, or scams, or ask anyone to pay through Localites;
- post someone else's personal details without their permission;
- post anything false or misleading, including fake reviews or fake bookings;
- post anything you don't have the right to share, such as someone else's photos;
- try to see information that isn't yours, get around our limits or security, or slow down
  or harm the site; or
- copy listings or other content from the site in bulk, by automated means or otherwise.

## 5. Planning events and inviting guests

- Your events, plans and notes are private to you. See the privacy policy for exactly what a
  business sees when you mark it as booked.
- When you add guests, you're responsible for having their permission to use their name and
  contact details. We email a guest only when you change their reply.
- Anyone with a group invitation link can reply through it. Share it only where you're happy
  for that to happen; you can switch it off or replace it at any time.

## 6. Messages and files

- Conversations are private between you and the business. The Localites team reads a
  conversation only when someone reports it.
- Only photos and PDFs can be sent, up to 10 MB each. Files are deleted after a while (see
  the privacy policy) and aren't scanned for viruses, so only open files from people you
  trust.
- Don't share sensitive information you don't need to.
- You can block someone or report a message or conversation at any time.
- Businesses can't start conversations; they can only reply to people who contact them.

## 7. Businesses on Localites

If you claim or run a business page, you also agree that:

- you're the business's owner, or have its permission, and the details you give us when
  claiming are true;
- your page, photos and replies are accurate and follow section 4, and you have the right to
  use every photo you add;
- what you write on your page and in replies to reviews may be checked for offensive
  language before it shows, and held for our team to decide (see the privacy policy);
- you'll use the information people share with you (names, event summaries, messages) only
  to respond to them and provide your services, and not for marketing they didn't ask for;
- you won't offer anything in exchange for a review, or ask people to change or remove a
  review (see the Review Policy); and
- the business owner is responsible for who is on the business's team. Managers can do what
  the owner can, except manage the team.

- Use **Report** on a message, conversation or review, or email supportlocalites@gmail.com.
